# Create Aestro Quest Book

A step-by-step quest book for Create and its addons in the Create Aestro modpack.

**Open it here:** https://spekac007.github.io/create-aestro-questbook/

- 180 short quests in 13 chapters, from your first andesite to planes and submarines
- Farm guides and tips for contraptions, drills and trains
- "Show recipe" pop-ups with the real recipes from the pack
- Your progress is saved in your browser. Use **Back up your progress** at the bottom of the page to keep a code you can paste back later or on another computer.

The page updates itself: when a new version is uploaded here, you get it the next time you open the page, and your progress stays.
